A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Have something else on your mind?

What can you print?+

Our specialties include flex printing, banners, cards, boards, tags, ID cards, T-shirts and cups or mugs. If you have another format in mind, tell us about it and we'll discuss what's possible.

Do I need a print-ready design?+

Share what you have, whether it's finished artwork or an early idea. We'll review your requirements and discuss the next steps, including artwork preparation, before confirming your project.

How much will my project cost?+

Pricing depends on your format, quantity, materials and finishing. Use our project-brief builder to outline what you need, then send it to us on LinkedIn for a tailored discussion and quote.

How long will printing take?+

Timing varies with the product and order size. Let us know your preferred deadline when you enquire, and we'll confirm a schedule before your order goes ahead.

Where can I find Printzone?+

We're based in New Delhi, Delhi 110017, with a location in Mehsana, Gujarat. Connect with us on LinkedIn to arrange a visit or discuss your project.
